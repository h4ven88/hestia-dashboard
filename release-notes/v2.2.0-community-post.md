# v2.2.0 is out, and it is a security release

Chasing the cloud sync problems two of you reported this week turned up
something worse than the problem itself. So this release is mostly security,
plus the fixes for what you actually hit.

**Update both halves:** re-paste `HestiaDashboard.groovy`, then open the app and
click **⬇ Update Dashboard File**. You do not need to click Done this time.

## The one that matters

Hestia stores your settings in the cloud keyed by your public IP address. If
your ISP puts you behind shared addressing, and many do, other households share
that key with you.

Two endpoints had no authentication. One listed your sensors, including your
smoke detectors. The other accepted events for them without checking who was
sending. Smoke alerts deliberately skip the armed check, because a fire matters
whether or not you armed the house.

Chained together, somebody sharing your address could make every phone in your
house say "Smoke / CO Alert" at three in the morning, as often as they liked.
They could also read when your doors opened, add their phone to your
notifications, or delete your devices so your alarms stopped arriving.

All closed. No sign any of it was ever used, but it was possible.

## What you reported

- **Your household could re-key itself and lock you out.** A device that could
  not reach the companion app invented its own household key, which happens
  routinely on hestari.com. Three guards now stop it, including one in the hub
  app that catches it even from older dashboards.
- **You can enter a household key on a device that still works**, in Settings →
  Diagnostics. It checks the key against your real record before saving it.
- **Regenerating your Maker API token no longer locks you out forever.**
- **Diagnostics names the real cause now.** Opening the dashboard from your
  hub's own address cannot reach the cloud, which used to show up as
  "hestari.com unreachable". It now tells you what is actually unavailable from
  that address, and what is unaffected: your hub keeps firing reminders, and
  push still reaches devices already registered.

One to watch for: if a write is rejected as unauthorised, **alarm and arm/disarm
notifications stop while door and window ones keep arriving**. That looks like a
working system and is not. Diagnostics now says so.

## Changeover

Only a browser can register the new webhook, so the old form is still accepted
**until 2026-11-30** rather than cutting off households that rarely open the
dashboard. Yours switches over by itself the first time you open it after
updating. If you have updated and not opened it, open it once.

## If you have had cloud sync trouble this past week

A few of you did, and it is worth saying plainly what it was.

v2.0.0 moved everyone onto encrypted settings with real write authorisation,
which is what stops a neighbour reading your tokens and PINs. The cost of
authorising writes is that it becomes possible to lose the thing that authorises
you, and the routes back in were not all finished when that shipped. Two
households got caught in that window.

**This is a transition problem, not an ongoing one.** From 2.2.0, a regenerated
Maker API token recovers on its own, a device that cannot reach your hub no
longer invents its own household key, and the hub itself refuses to let one
device re-key the rest of the house. New installs will not hit any of it, and
neither will anyone who updates before it happens to them.

The one case 2.2.0 cannot undo is a household **already** locked out before
updating, because the new recovery route did not exist when that record was
written. If that is you, say so in the thread and I will sort it out
individually. Do not start over, and do not re-enter your devices.
