🎉 Hestia v1.5.8 — 6 new wake words

Hey everyone,

Quick one this release — v1.5.8 is all about expanding your wake word options. Alongside Athena, Artemis, and Hestia, you can now pick from:

**Stable (recommended):**
- Odin
- Achilles
- Andromeda

**Experimental (works, but less reliable — flagged in the picker so you know what you're getting):**
- Apollo
- Hermes
- Anubis

Head to **Settings → Athena → Wake Word** to switch. Each word runs its own locally-trained detection model, so give it a few tries after switching — the first load pulls down a small model file.

One note: I also trained an Osiris model, but it just didn't perform well enough to ship (barely detected at all), so I pulled it rather than give you a wake word that doesn't work. Might revisit it later with more training data.

As always, let me know if you run into issues — screenshots or console logs help a ton if something's not detecting reliably.

—Haven
