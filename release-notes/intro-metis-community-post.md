**A small note on how support here works now**

From today I am establishing AI support for Hestia, to help me keep up with
the questions and issues raised here. It is called Metis, after the Titaness
of counsel and Athena's mother, which keeps it in the same family as the rest
of the house.

What it does: it watches this thread, reads new posts against the actual code,
and drafts a reply for me. I read every one before it goes up, and I post them
myself.

What it does not do: post on its own. It has no account here and I am not
giving it one. Anything you read from this account is something I read first
and decided to send.

Replies that are substantively its words rather than mine will say so at the
bottom. Release notes and anything where I am making a judgement call stay
mine.

The point is just that I stop missing posts. I work a day job, and a few of
you have waited longer than you should have for an answer. This should make
that quicker without making it worse.

If you would rather hear from me directly on something, say so and you will.
