# v2.0.2 forum post

**v2.0.2 is up. Take this one if you use cloud sync.**

I introduced a bug in v2.0.0 that could wipe your settings across every device in your home. @jbwrd3 hit it over the past few days. Nothing he did caused it.

**What went wrong.** v2.0.0 started encrypting your settings with a key belonging to your household. What I missed is that the old weak key was also how a device *bootstrapped itself*. So a device that could not reach your hub had no way in, Hestia assumed it was a brand new household, and showed the setup wizard. Finishing that wizard rebuilt rooms from scratch and created a *new* household key, which locked out every other device. They would then get the wizard too. One device starting fresh took the whole household with it.

The tell was Diagnostics showing **"Record present but unreadable"**.

**What it does now.** Hestia tells the difference between a new household and an existing one it cannot read. When it finds settings it cannot decrypt it no longer offers the wizard, it explains and points at the fix.

**Already stuck? You do not need the update to recover.** Open Hestia on a device that still works → **Settings → Wall Panel** → open that link on the stuck device. It carries your household key across.

**One correction to my earlier instructions.** Use the **⬇ Update Dashboard File** button, not Done. Done runs a conditional download that can silently skip and leave your hub on the old dashboard.
