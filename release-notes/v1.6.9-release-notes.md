# Hestia v1.6.9 Release Notes

**Release Date:** 2026-09-20

---

## Garage doors are their own thing now

Until this release Hestia had no idea what a garage door was. If you added one, it was stored as a light switch, and that had consequences well beyond looking wrong.

**It counted as a light, and "all lights on" or "all lights off" sent it an on/off command.** On a relay-style opener, which is how most Hubitat garage setups work, `on` is exactly the command that moves the door. Turning on all your lights could open your garage. If you have a garage door in Hestia, this release is worth taking.

### What you get

A garage door now has its own control, in **Security** underneath your locks, where the rest of your doors live. It also appears in its room if you've assigned it to one, and the two stay in step.

Each door shows Closed, Open, Opening, Closing, or Unknown, using the garage icon from Artemis's own icon set. While the door is genuinely moving, the panel in the icon travels up or down with it.

### Opening asks first

Closing is one tap, because closing is the safe direction. **Opening takes two:** the button arms itself, says "Confirm open", and gives up after five seconds if you don't confirm. It's a button rather than a popup dialog, so it still works on kiosk browsers that suppress dialogs.

By voice, closing is immediate and opening asks out loud: "Open the garage door? Say yes to confirm." A wake word can be set off by a television or someone outside a window, so the one command that opens your house waits for an answer. That check lives in Hestia itself, not in the AI's instructions, so it can't be talked around.

**Routines can close a garage but never open one.** A routine can run on a schedule with nobody home, and nothing there can confirm an intent to open a door.

### It understands the hardware

Openers differ, and Hestia now drives each one the way it expects:

- A proper garage door device gets `open` and `close`.
- A relay gets a single pulse, and because that same pulse also opens the door, those openers confirm in **both** directions and show one honest "Move door" button rather than a fake Open/Close pair.
- A momentary opener gets `push`.
- Hardware that reports no way to be operated is told to you, rather than being sent a command that silently does nothing.

### It won't claim a door is shut when it isn't

"Closed" is only ever shown when your hub actually says closed. A door reporting an unknown or unrecognised state says **Unknown** and offers both buttons. Some Hubitat drivers are known to get stuck reporting "opening" forever; after 45 seconds Hestia stops believing it and says Unknown instead.

### Finding your door

Any device declaring the standard garage capabilities is detected automatically, whatever the brand. Relay openers don't declare those, so they're matched on a name containing both "garage" and door/opener, with lights, fans and sensors deliberately excluded. Anything missed can be set by hand in Settings, which now offers "Garage Door" in the device type list.

If you already had a garage door saved as a switch, Hestia moves it out of your lights automatically, but only when the device's own capabilities prove it's a door. A switch merely *named* "garage light" is left exactly where it is.

### A note for myQ owners

Chamberlain closed the myQ API to third parties in late 2023, which is why Hubitat's myQ integrations stopped working. There's no safe way for Hestia to work around that, and storing your Chamberlain password to try would be worse than the problem. Local hardware such as ratgdo or the Konnected GDO blaQ replaces the cloud path, presents an ordinary device to Hubitat, and is picked up by Hestia automatically. Check your opener's learn button first: white means Security+ 3.0, which ratgdo doesn't support.

## Also in this release

- Assigning a newly discovered garage door, colour bulb or CT bulb from Staging no longer silently discards it.
- Importing a settings backup taken before a device type existed no longer breaks Settings.
- Removed a dead sensor-row builder that no longer had any callers.
