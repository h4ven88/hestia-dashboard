v1.6.7 is out. This one is mostly about Artemis and your alarm system working together, plus a few things you've been asking for.

**Entry delay countdown.** When your system is armed and your alarm starts its entry delay, Hestia jumps to Security, shows a countdown ring, opens the PIN pad on your phone, and announces it on devices with Announcements on. If the countdown runs out and the siren goes off, it switches to the alarm screen. Disarm from Hestia, your keypad, or the Ring app and it clears.

**Driven by your alarm system.** The countdown comes from what HSM or Ring actually reports, not from guessing based on a door opening. Your alarm decides which doors and modes start a delay, and Hestia follows it. On Ring, the countdown uses Ring's own timer, so it matches your keypad.

**HSM and Ring stay in sync.** Arm Away, Arm Home and Disarm in Hestia now go to both HSM and Ring at the same time, so you don't need rules to keep them matched. If you already have rules for that, they keep working alongside it.

**Official and community Ring integrations.** Hestia now works with both, including the community integration's Ring Virtual Alarm Hub. If you have both installed, it uses whichever one is actually connected to your alarm.

**Spoken arm and disarm announcements.** "Arming away, 60 seconds starting now," "Armed home," "Disarmed," on any device with Announcements turned on.

**Camera refresh interval.** Settings > Cameras > Refresh every, from 1 to 60 seconds, for both the grid and the enlarged view. Cameras with a Live Stream URL still play live when you enlarge them. Snapshot cameras now keep refreshing in the enlarged view too. @jkudave this one's for you.

**HTTPS hub addresses.** You can set your hub to https://[hub-ip] with no port and it sticks. If you open Hestia straight from your hub, it connects back the same way you loaded it.

**Reconnect the companion app.** If the Companion App check in Diagnostics fails on hestari.com, for example after reinstalling the Hestia app on your hub, there's now a Reconnect option. Open the link it shows, copy that page, paste it in, and you're set. It checks with your hub before saving anything.

**To update:**

1. Update via Hubitat Package Manager, or re-paste HestiaDashboard.groovy under Apps Code
2. Open the Hestia app on your hub and click Done

**Ring users:** go to Apps > Maker API and make sure your alarm base station is selected, not just your sensors. On the community integration that device is called **Ring Virtual Alarm Hub**. It doesn't show up as a tile anywhere, so it's easy to miss. Without it Hestia can't see the countdown or send it commands.

**HSM users:** Hestia's countdown uses the Entry Delay set in Artemis settings, so keep it matched to your HSM delay.

**Using HTTPS on more than one device?** Each phone, tablet and computer has to open https://[hub-ip] once and click through the certificate warning before Hestia can connect from it.
