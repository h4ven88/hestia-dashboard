**Release Date:** 2026-07-22

## Bug Fixes

### Garage door dual-capability detection
- **Fixed:** Garage door relays that also report a paired contact sensor (e.g. Zooz Zen17 with a physical door sensor wired to its S1/S2 input) were being misclassified as a plain contact sensor instead of a garage door. This caused the same physical door to show up twice under Artemis "Doors" (once from the real sensor, once from the relay) with no way to control it as a switch.
- **Root cause:** `capToType()` checked for `ContactSensor` capability before the name-based garage fallback, so any device reporting both capabilities never reached the garage check. Separately, `detectNewDevices()` excluded any security-capable device from general staging without exempting `garage` the way it already exempted `lock`.
- **Fix:** Reordered the name-based garage fallback ahead of the `ContactSensor` check, and added `garage` to the security-capability staging exemption alongside `lock`.
- Reported by jbwrd3 on the [Hubitat Community forum](https://community.hubitat.com/t/hestia-a-new-smart-home-dashboard-for-hubitat/162853/143).

## Wake Word Models

- **Hestia** — retrained on the official recipe (50k/50k/3.5k/aug2 + real clip injection). Detection rate improved from 39.0% to 70.1% on the benchmark set, with false activations remaining within target (<1% negative, <6% hard negative).
- **Athena** — promoted production model finalized from the prior training round.
