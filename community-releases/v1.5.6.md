## Hestia v1.5.6 - International Weather, Fan+Light Combos, and More

Hey everyone, v1.5.6 is live. Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) to pick it up.

---

### International Weather (Open-Meteo)

The weather module has been completely rebuilt. The previous US-only stack (NWS + ZIP code) has been replaced with [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) -- a free, international weather API that requires no API key.

- **City search replaces ZIP code** -- Settings now has a "City or Location" field. Type a city name, pick from proximity-ranked results showing city, state/region, and country.
- **Works worldwide** -- weather data is available for any location, not just US ZIP codes.
- **Day/night forecasts restored** -- forecast now shows half-day periods (Tonight, Tomorrow, Tomorrow Night, etc.) instead of just daily summaries.
- **Precipitation probability** -- rain/snow chance shown with a raindrop icon when above 20%.
- **Dark theme icon fix** -- weather SVGs now properly visible on all dark themes.

---

### Fan + Light Combo Devices

For those with combo switches like the Inovelli Blue that control both a fan and a light from one device -- you can now link the light side through Settings.

- **Dedicated fan switches** (GE, Zooz, etc.) remain plain fans. No auto-splitting.
- On any fan in **Settings > Devices**, click **"+ Add light control"** to link the light side as a Dimmer or Switch.
- The linked light appears in Dimmers or Switches with a "Linked from fan" label.
- Managed entirely from the Fans section -- no duplication.

---

### Temperature Unit Toggle

Three display modes: Fahrenheit, Celsius (label only), and Celsius (converted from F). Applied across the entire dashboard -- home screen, climate card, room views, ribbon, sidebar, and Athena voice context.

Settings location: **Settings > Thermostats > Temperature Display Unit**

---

### Auto-Hide Empty Categories

If you don't have fans, blinds, or thermostats, those cards and nav items are now hidden automatically. No configuration needed -- the UI adapts based on your device inventory.

---

### Bug Fixes

- **Theme contrast** -- all 5 themes updated to meet WCAG AA contrast ratios
- **Security as default room** -- Artemis now appears in the Default Room dropdown when enabled
- **Config sync** -- reverted to hub-is-truth model to prevent stale configs from overwriting local changes
